<!-- The join target. national_census_records resolves polling_place_id against these exact columns. -->

`polling_places` unique key columns (all `unsignedSmallInteger`):
  dane_department_code, dane_municipality_code, zone_code, place_code
Unique index name: `polling_places_divipole_unique`.
`PollingPlace` model $fillable includes: department_id, municipality_id, dane_department_code, dane_municipality_code, zone_code, place_code, name, address, commune, max_tables.
`PollingPlace` already has: department() BelongsTo, municipality() BelongsTo — so department/municipality NAMES and `address` are reachable through the FK.

CSV layout (semicolon-delimited, 13 columns, header row present, ISO-8859-1, CRLF):
  idx0 divipol | idx1 codificado | idx2 cedula | idx3 dpto | idx4 cero | idx5 cero |
  idx6 mcpio | idx7 ref1 | idx8 zona | idx9 ref2 | idx10 puesto | idx11 nombre | idx12 mesa
Example: `280019909010,00;280019909;1100696116;28;0;0;1;;99;0;9;CHOCHO;10`
  → cedula=1100696116, dpto=28, mcpio=1, zona=99, puesto=9, nombre=CHOCHO, mesa=10
Join map: dpto→dane_department_code, mcpio→dane_municipality_code, zona→zone_code, puesto→place_code.

Reuse from PollingPlaceSeeder: the keyBy in-memory map + `flush()` chunked `upsert()` idiom (see that file's lines 38-51, 95-120).
