### AUTHZ-01 detail

AUTHZ-01 requires: *"Los widgets/exports/dashboards existentes que asumen que el coordinador es el tope de la jerarquía (p. ej. TopLeadersTable, TopLeadersExport, LeadersExportController) se actualizan para resolver correctamente el equipo transitivo de un articulador."*

All three named files **were** updated identically and correctly — `teamCoordinatorUserIds()` resolves an articulador's transitive team the same way in all three. Two of the three (`TopLeadersTable`, `TopLeadersExport`) are reachable by a logged-in articulador today via the widget on their own panel dashboard. The third, `LeadersExportController`, is bound to a route gated to `coordinator,admin_campaign,super_admin` with its only UI trigger living on the coordinador-exclusive `leaders.blade.php` page — an articulador has no page that links to it, because Phase 15 (per ARTIC-02's literal scope) only built a self-service *coordinadores* list for articulador, never a *líderes* list.

**Likely read:** this is not a functional defect so much as an artifact of AUTHZ-01 naming a coordinador-only UI surface as one of its "example" targets — the requirement's actual intent (transitive team resolves correctly wherever exposed) is satisfied, since the primary líder-facing surface an articulador *can* reach (`TopLeadersTable`/`TopLeadersExport`) works correctly. No requirement in this milestone (ARTIC-02 or otherwise) asked for an articulador-facing líderes list/export page.

**Recommendation:** proceed — treat as a documented scope note, not a blocker. If a future need arises for articuladores to bulk-export their transitive líderes list directly (rather than via the TopLeadersTable ranking export), add `area_coordinator` to the `coordinator.leaders.export` route's role middleware and surface a link from the articulador panel — small, well-scoped follow-up, not urgent.
