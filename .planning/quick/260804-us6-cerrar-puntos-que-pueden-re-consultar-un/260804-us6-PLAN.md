<objective>
Close two already-diagnosed gaps (no further investigation needed — both confirmed in the discussion that produced this plan) where a cédula already resolved in `registraduria_lookups` can still trigger a live (2captcha-costing) lookup against the Registraduría:

1. **"Actualizar datos" force-refresh** — legitimate for correcting stale data, but must become super_admin-only, enforced on the backend (not just hidden in the UI), on top of the confirmation modal that already exists.
2. **`RegistraduriaController::lookup()`** — an authenticated, likely-orphaned route that calls the Python microservice directly with no permanent-cache check at all.

Purpose: stop unnecessary 2captcha spend and unauthorized re-triggering of live lookups on cédulas already resolved, while leaving every other part of the cascade (automatic reconciliation, the normal "Consultar Registraduría" button) untouched — they already respect the cache.

Output: a real backend authorization guard (not just a hidden button), and a real cache-check guard on the orphaned controller route, both covered by Pest tests.
</objective>
