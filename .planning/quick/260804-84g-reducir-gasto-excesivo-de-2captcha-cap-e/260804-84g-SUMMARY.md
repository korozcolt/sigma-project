**Capped/exhaustion-tracked DispatchCensusRevalidation (shared Voter counter with ReconcileFallbackPollingPlaces) and widened PollingPlaceResolver's automated-cascade polling window from ~2.6s to ~40s.**

## Accomplishments
- `DispatchCensusRevalidation` now hard-caps at 50 voters/run and excludes already-exhausted voters, reusing `Voter::reconciliation_attempts`/`reconciliation_exhausted_at` (the same columns `ReconcileFallbackPollingPlaces` already uses) instead of retrying a never-resolving voter forever every hour.
- `PollingPlaceResolver::attemptLiveAutomated()` now polls up to 9 times with 5s sleeps (~40s total) per adapter instead of ~2.6s, giving the real 2captcha-backed `registraduria-service` microservice (own 5s polling tick, up to 150s worst case) a realistic chance to finish before the automated cascade abandons it and moves to the next adapter/tier.

## Decisions Made
- Reused `Voter`'s existing `reconciliation_attempts`/`reconciliation_exhausted_at` columns for `DispatchCensusRevalidation` rather than adding a new column/counter — both jobs ultimately trigger the same `PollingPlaceResolver::resolveAutomated()` cascade for the same `document_number`, so "attempts to resolve this voter automatically" is one shared concept regardless of which hourly job ran it. This also means a genuinely unresolvable voter stops being double-processed (once per job, per hour) once either job marks it exhausted.
- Widened `attemptLiveAutomated`'s poll budget via named constants (`LIVE_POLL_ATTEMPTS`, `LIVE_POLL_INTERVAL_MS`) instead of a raw array literal, matching the sibling job's constant-based style and making the ~40s/adapter budget self-documenting.
